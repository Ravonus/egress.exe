SELECT commands the equipped critter; it never creates a new individual.

Ordinary critter death is permanent. Vine Frog alone currently carries `second_spring`: one self-revival at half health, with a spent flag belonging to that individual. Paired CRC journals tie the individual records to the matching floor checkpoint. Death updates that matching checkpoint individual without copying newly collected ingredients onto an unmodified room. Duplicate species remain separate individuals.

Medicine heals the heir first, including a missing quarter heart. When the heir is completely healthy it heals a living critter. It remains on the ground when both are healthy, and cannot revive a dead critter. It does not obstruct crates, falling loads or living obstacles. No-combat puzzle proofs ignore medical decoration because health is outside that solver's state.

## Environmental actions under native acceptance

Fire turns planted or natural foliage into a charred stump/ash state, which accepts another seed. Cold affects a bounded patch of natural water (five cells at radius one, thirteen at radius two); heat thaws ice. The authored apparatus retains its existing connected puzzle reactions. A two-cell frost ray cannot jump an intervening shore or wall, and growth waits while a body occupies its target.

The shared `eg_environment` matrix owns the state transitions. Material plus skin are stored together in the existing persistent packet store; gravity's transient high bits are never repurposed. Anticipation, elemental particles, rising smoke, named audio and a spent phase surround the material transition. Seed, sprout, tree and ash each have native art/idle poses in four existing card slots. Both mapper builds pass the 21-assertion native environment route; website/browser acceptance also passes for the same ROM pair.
