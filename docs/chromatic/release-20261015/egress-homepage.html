WHAT THIS IS

EGRESS.EXE IS A GAME BOY COLOR CARTRIDGE. TEN FLOORS. ONE DOOR EACH. EXITS ARE TICKETS. FILE ONE. GO DOWN.

  • DIG THROUGH THE CARPET. THE CRTS ABOVE YOU FALL WHEN NOTHING HOLDS THEM UP. EVERY FLOOR WAS SOLVED BEFORE YOU SAW IT.
  • FIGHT WITH ONE LASH: A THREE-HIT COMBO, A CHARGED SPIN, A DODGE ROLL.
  • TALK TO WHOEVER WAITS ON THE LANDINGS. SIX FACTIONS. NONE OF THEM AGREE ABOUT WHAT HAPPENED.
  • GO DOWN. THE TENTH FLOOR IS WATCHED. THE ENDING IS A QUESTION.

HOW TO PLAY

I WROTE IT ALL DOWN FOR YOU. YOU WILL NOT READ IT. I WROTE THAT DOWN TOO.

THE FLOOR
THE D-PAD WALKS AND DIGS. LEAN INTO A CRT TO PUSH IT. CRTS FALL, ROLL OFF EACH OTHER AND HURT. FIND THE KEYCARDS, THEN THE DOOR.
STUCK?
HOLD SELECT TO RESET THE FLOOR. IT COSTS HALF A HEART.
A FIGHT
A LASHES (THREE HITS). HOLD A TO CHARGE, LET GO TO SPIN. B ROLLS THROUGH DANGER.
LANDINGS
AFTER FLOORS 3, 6 AND 9 SOMEONE TALKS. YOUR ANSWER MOVES WHAT IS TRUE, AND WHAT THEY CHARGE YOU.
THE END
FLOOR 10. DROP THE GALLERY'S CRTS ON IT WHILE IT IS STUNNED.
ON A KEYBOARD
ARROWS WALK. X OR A IS A. Z OR S IS B. ENTER IS START. SHIFT IS SELECT.
