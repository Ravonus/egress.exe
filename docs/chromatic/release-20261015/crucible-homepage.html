WHAT THIS IS

CRUCIBLE.EXE IS A GAME BOY COLOR CARTRIDGE. YOU PUT TWO THINGS TOGETHER. SOMETHING ELSE COMES OUT. THEN THE ROOM CHANGES.

  • MAKE THOUSANDS OF THINGS, TWO AT A TIME.
  • TALK OR FIGHT WHEN FACES COME TO THE BENCH. THEY ASK, AND REMEMBER WHAT YOU MADE THEM.
  • WAKE UP IN A STORY YOU DO NOT REMEMBER STARTING. IT IS NOT THE SAME TWICE.
  • LINK TWO CARTRIDGES WITH ONE CABLE.
